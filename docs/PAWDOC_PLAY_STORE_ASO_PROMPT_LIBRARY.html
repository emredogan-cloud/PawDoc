1 · The non-negotiable rules

These are in every prompt below, per asset, as instructions — not as a preamble. The FormAI post-mortem's central finding was that a rules section at the top of a document does not constrain a generator: "The rules were right and the assets violated them anyway."

Device

  • A modern bezel-less Android handset, small centred punch-hole camera.
  • Hard No notch, no Dynamic Island, no iOS status bar, no Apple device, no smartwatch.
  • Status bar reads a neutral time, never 9:41 (Apple's marketing time).

Health claims

  • Hard No diagnosis, no named condition, no cause.
  • Hard No score, grade, percentage or meter about the animal.
  • Hard No "fine", "healthy", "all good", "excellent", "normal".
  • No X-ray, skeleton, anatomical overlay, or wireframe animal.
  • No red cross emblem — it is a protected mark, and it implies a clinic.

AI

  • AI is labelled AI, and looks synthetic. Never a human face, never a person's name.
  • Hard No confidence figure, ever. The app never shows one.
  • No "accurate", "reliable", "you can trust", "clinically", "science-backed".

Veterinary

  • Hard No vet is employed, contracted, verified or reachable. Never imply one.
  • No stethoscope, white coat, clinic room or scrubs as a PawDoc signifier.
  • "Made by vets", "vet-approved", "vet chat" — never.

Social proof

  • Hard No star rating, review count, download count, user count, award or "#1".
  • No human face presented as a user. Avatars are abstract monograms.
  • PawDoc is pre-launch. Every one of these would be fabricated.

Money

  • Hard No price, no trial length, no discount, no "free trial" — none is configured in Play.
  • No money-back guarantee. PawDoc runs no refund programme.
  • "Free" may only describe what is genuinely free forever (see §2).

Privacy

  • Hard No absolute: "100% private", "never shared", "stays on your device".
  • Every privacy line names its scope. Photos are uploaded, to Cloudflare R2, with EXIF/GPS stripped first.
  • Nothing may contradict the Data safety declaration.

Format

  • 1080 × 1920, 9:16, PNG, no transparency Hard
  • No border, no rounded canvas corners, no drop shadow on the canvas.
  • ≤ 8 MB per screenshot. Same dimensions across the whole set.

4 · Store metadata

App name 30 char limit

PawDoc: Pet Health Checker

26 / 30. Carries three ranking terms — pet, health, checker — and describes the action without claiming diagnosis. The current metadata file says PawDoc: AI Pet Health (21), which spends four characters on "AI" and none on what the app does.

Alternatives, if a different term tests better: PawDoc: Dog & Cat Health (24) — species keywords, weaker verb · PawDoc: Pet Symptom Checker (27) — strongest search match, but "symptom checker" is a medical-adjacent category term; keep it only if you are comfortable defending it under Health Content & Services.

Short description 80 char limit

Describe a symptom, get a clear next step. Emergency help works offline.

72 / 80. ⚠ The short description currently in docs/store_metadata/google_play.md is 144 characters and cannot be saved in the Console — the file's own table claims "72 ✓", which was never recomputed after the copy was rewritten. Count characters, do not estimate them.

Full description 4000 char limit

The pet health record your vet actually wants to see.

When your dog goes off their food or your cat starts acting strange, the hard
part isn't knowing that something is off. It's knowing how soon it matters,
and remembering the details three days later in an exam room.

PawDoc helps with both.

DESCRIBE IT, AND GET A NEXT STEP
Type what you're seeing, or add a photo. You get one of four clear answers —
get help now, call your vet today, book a routine visit, or watch and re-check
— with a timeframe, and a specific list of signs that mean it's time to
escalate.

PawDoc will never tell you your pet is fine. It tells you what to watch for
and when to act. When there isn't enough information to say, it says that
instead of guessing.

EMERGENCY HELP, FREE AND OFFLINE
A red button on every screen. It works with no signal and no account: five
step-by-step first-aid guides (choking, heavy bleeding, seizure, a swollen
hard belly, overheating), one tap to find a nearby emergency vet in your maps
app, and tap-to-dial poison control.

Emergency help is free on every plan, is never counted against any allowance,
and is never behind a payment screen. That is enforced on our servers, not
just in the app.

THE RECORD DOES THE REMEMBERING
• Every check saved to your pet's timeline automatically
• Weight, medications, vaccinations and vet visits in one history
• Re-check and medication reminders, delivered by your own phone
• Vet Visit Prep: your reasons, your questions and the record, in one place
• Unlimited pets, unlimited records, on every plan

ASK THE ASSISTANT
Everyday pet-life questions — food, behaviour, travel, what to expect at a
first vaccination. Answers come from an AI model, are labelled as such, and
are general information rather than veterinary advice.

WHAT'S FREE, FOREVER
Emergency help. Symptom checks by text, with no limit. Every pet, every health
record, every reminder, the vet-prep pack and the timeline. An optional
Premium subscription lifts three counted allowances (photo checks, journal
entries, assistant messages) and adds a printable PDF health report. Price and
terms are shown by Google Play before you buy anything, and you can cancel in
Google Play at any time.

ABOUT YOUR DATA
Your account, your pets and your records are stored on our servers, tied to
your account and sent over an encrypted connection. Photos you attach to a
check are uploaded so they can be looked at — location and camera metadata are
stripped from them on your phone first. You can delete your account and
everything in it from inside the app, in two taps. We do not sell your data
and there are no ads.

WHAT PAWDOC IS NOT
PawDoc is an information and record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device.
It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, it does not name
what your pet has, and it is not a substitute for examination by a licensed
veterinarian. No veterinarian reviews what it tells you. If you think this is
an emergency, contact a veterinarian or an emergency animal hospital right
now — do not wait on an app.

≈ 2,750 characters. Structure is verb-first feature blocks, each traceable to one shipped area, closing on the two honesty sections. That structure is what makes the claim audit cheap: one heading, one feature, one file.

The final paragraph is deliberate. Play's Health Content & Services policy asks non-medical-device health apps for a clear in-description disclaimer that the app "is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition." The wording above says exactly that.

5 · Keyword strategy

Google Play has no keyword field. Ranking comes from the title, the short description, and term frequency in the long description — all user-visible. So terms are placed inside sentences a human will read, and the list below was derived from the copy above rather than injected into it. That ordering is the anti-stuffing control.

18 · Localization notes

Ship en-US only for the first release. The app itself falls back to English for every non-English, non-German locale, so a Turkish listing would send Turkish users to an English app — the divergence a locale audit is meant to catch.

When a second locale is added:

  • Localization changes language, not product truth. Same features, same numbers, same slots, same disclaimers, same allowances. One global icon — icons are not localizable.
  • Generate the artwork with no non-ASCII text and typeset the locale afterwards. No prompt reliably prevents diacritic decay: a previous project shipped CANLI ANALIZ for CANLI ANALİZ, güveiinş for güven, and AI TEKNOLO.iSi where the J became a full stop. In Turkish, ı/i and I/İ are different letters.
  • Number formats invert. 28.4 kg in en-US is 28,4 kg in tr-TR; 87% is %87. Both directions leak.
  • Never let a generator draw the wordmark in any locale. Composite it.
  • Diff every asset against its twin for contaminated rows — content migrates between frames when a set is regenerated in one session, and neither a policy check nor a design check catches it.
